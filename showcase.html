01Data & insight

Business Intelligence & Data Visualisation

University Assessment | Power BI · Excel · DAX

Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI report examining UK CO₂ emissions and population trends between 1990 and 2023. Worked with two Excel datasets containing 7,585 emissions records and 232 country records, organising data and developing analytical visualisations to communicate key trends.

My Role

Prepared and structured datasets, developed data relationships and DAX measures, and created report visuals covering UK annual emissions, emissions by fuel source, annual reductions, emissions per person, population changes and comparisons with Germany and France.

Emissions dataset records
7,585
Country dataset records
232

Key Findings

UK CO₂ emissions fell substantially between 1990 and 2023, reaching approximately 307.83 million tonnes in 2023, or 4.48 tonnes per person. Emissions declined despite UK population growth, while coal-related emissions fell markedly. The country comparison showed that the UK's absolute 2023 emissions were lower than Germany's but higher than France's. These national trends provide context rather than measurements of the NHS Trust's own emissions.
