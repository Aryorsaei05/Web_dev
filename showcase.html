01Data & insight

Business Intelligence & Data Visualisation

University Assessment | Power BI · Excel · DAX

Project Overview

The project addressed the need to make a large emissions dataset easier to explore and compare. Developed an interactive Power BI report examining UK CO₂ emissions and population trends between 1990 and 2023. Worked with two Excel datasets containing 7,585 emissions records and 232 country records, organising data and developing analytical visualisations to communicate key trends.

My Role

Prepared and structured datasets, developed data relationships and DAX measures, and created report visuals covering UK annual emissions, emissions by fuel source, annual reductions, emissions per person, population changes and comparisons with Germany and France.

Emissions dataset records
7,585
Country dataset records
232

Key Findings

UK CO₂ emissions fell substantially between 1990 and 2023, reaching approximately 307.83 million tonnes in 2023, or 4.48 tonnes per person. Emissions declined despite UK population growth, while coal-related emissions fell markedly. The country comparison showed that the UK's absolute 2023 emissions were lower than Germany's but higher than France's. These national trends provide context rather than measurements of the NHS Trust's own emissions.

Outcome: An interactive report brings trends, country comparisons and emissions-per-person measures into one place. It supports exploration of the supplied data; no real-world NHS emissions reduction or operational impact is claimed.

UK Charity Sector Research & Outreach Strategy

PurposeTech.ai | Analytics & Research Internship | Microsoft Excel

During my Analytics & Research Internship at PurposeTech.ai, conducted research into the UK charity grant-funding market to understand fundraising challenges, digital adoption and opportunities for more targeted stakeholder engagement. Structured the findings across four Excel worksheets, connecting secondary research to practical charity segmentation and outreach planning.

Key Findings / Secondary Research

Findings drawn from the consulted sources and recorded in the workbook; these are not results from an independently conducted charity survey.

  • Fundraising capacity

    Sector research highlighted limited staff and resources at smaller charities, making funding research and repeated applications particularly demanding.

  • Application friction

    Grant discovery, eligibility checks and differing funder requirements contribute to repetitive administrative work.

  • Funding pressure

    Charities Aid Foundation reported estimated public donations of £15.4 billion in 2024 and £14 billion in 2025 (2025, 2026). This provides context for the project's focus on fundraising pressures.

  • AI adoption

    The workbook used interim 2026 research reporting 88% AI adoption. The final Charity Digital Skills Report (2026) revised the figure to 79%, compared with 76% in 2025, covering exploring, active and strategic adoption. The interim figure should not be treated as the final sector estimate or a measure of everyday use.

  • Existing market tools

    GrantFinder (n.d.) helps users find funding opportunities, while 360Giving (n.d.) provides searchable data about grants already awarded. Their different purposes matter when considering which tools could support prospect research.

03Restaurant digital transformation

Ember & Grain: from enquiry to email draft

University Assessment | Fictional local restaurant scenario | HTML · CSS · JavaScript

  • 6 Booking form fields: name, email, date, time, guests, message
  • 30 min Time slots generated from the opening hours
  • Preview Email draft shown for review before sending
  • Copy Fallback for visitors without a configured email app

Role & problem

The assessment role is CTO: turn a restaurant's communication needs into a usable web prototype. The page brings business context, a contact route, social sharing and structured booking requests into a single experience.

Page structure

The introduction explains the concept and its demonstration status. Contact and sharing links precede the form. Visitors then enter a name, email, date, time, guest count and optional message, inspect the generated draft and choose whether to open an email app or copy it.

Implementation

Hand-written HTML and external CSS provide labelled controls and a responsive two-column layout. JavaScript prevents page submission, checks the input, encodes the email subject and body, and generates a copyable preview. Editing an input clears a stale draft.

Outcome & limitations

The deliverable is an email-draft prototype, with no database, payment processing or booking confirmation. A visible copy fallback supports visitors without a configured email app. The concept uses the portfolio author's address and sharing actions; a real launch would require restaurant-owned contact details, social profiles and an agreed enquiry-handling process.
