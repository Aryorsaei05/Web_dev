02 / Business context

The challenge.

A local restaurant wants to strengthen its digital presence, make customer communication more accessible and simplify booking enquiries.

In this proposed assessment scenario, I take the role of Chief Technology Officer (CTO), planning a website prototype that brings essential information and customer enquiries together.

06 / Design rationale

Why this design works.

  • Clear navigation. Familiar links and a direct route to the enquiry form reduce friction.
  • Responsive layout. Stacked mobile sections, readable text and generous tap targets improve accessibility.
  • Visible contact information. A dedicated contact area makes communication easier.
  • A simple enquiry form. One clear interface reduces unnecessary steps.
  • Consistent hierarchy. Editorial typography, gold accents and considered spacing make information easier to find.
