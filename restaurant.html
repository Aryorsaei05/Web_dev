03 / Restaurant digital transformation

Ember & Grain.

A warmer welcome, from the first enquiry.

Wood-fired kitchen · Ancoats, Manchester

Business context

This fictional Manchester restaurant concept explores the CTO's task of improving customer communication: put essential information in one place, make the concept easy to share, and turn a booking request into a structured email.

Case study note. Ember & Grain is a fictional business. Its address, telephone number, opening hours and social media handles are placeholders. Booking enquiries go to the student's own email address; this website makes no booking and does not send or store any message.

Design choices

Business context and contact details come before the enquiry, so visitors can understand the concept before entering information. Visible labels, required-field checks and a date limit help make requests complete. The email preview lets visitors inspect the result; a copy option supports visitors without a configured email app.

The layout switches to a single column on smaller screens. It uses the portfolio's existing colours and typography, standard form controls and a local JavaScript generator. No database or automated reservation confirmation is needed for this prototype.
