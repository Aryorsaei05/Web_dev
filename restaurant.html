02 / Business context

The challenge.

A local restaurant wants a more consistent digital customer experience: accessible restaurant information, clearly presented contact and social details, and booking enquiries that are easier to prepare.

In this proposed assessment scenario, I take the role of Chief Technology Officer (CTO), planning a website prototype that brings essential information and customer enquiries together.

University of Salford · Web App Development Assessment 1. This is a fictional business scenario, not a real client project.

06 / Design rationale

Designed with purpose.

  • Clear navigation. Familiar links and a direct route to the enquiry form reduce friction.
  • Responsive layout. Stacked mobile sections, readable text and generous tap targets improve accessibility.
  • Visible contact information. A dedicated contact area makes communication easier.
  • A simple enquiry form. One clear interface reduces unnecessary steps.
  • Consistent hierarchy. Ivory serif headings establish visual hierarchy; gold details and burgundy actions create a consistent brand identity and make information easier to find.
